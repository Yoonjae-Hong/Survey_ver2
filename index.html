Instruction
  • [Step 1] Add rooms using the menu on the left and drag them into position to design your ideal floor plan.
    • The human icon represents a height of 180 cm for scale.
    • To remove a room/fixture, select it and press the Delete key.
  • [Step 2] Outline the boundary of your floor plan using the drawing tool.
    • Select Start Drawing to begin and select a corner of your floor plan.
    • Moving around your floor plan, select each corner to create a border.
    • To finish your outline, close your shape by ensuring that the last point is the same as your first point.
    • To remove an outline, select it and press Delete key.
  • [Step 3] Describe your home layout in the Text Panel on the right.
  • [Step 4] Press Save All to download PNG, JSON, and TXT files, and upload them to the Google Form.
