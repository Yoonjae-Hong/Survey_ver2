Instructions: Create the floor plan of your ideal one-story home.
  • [Step 1] Add rooms using the menu on the left and drag them into position.
  • [Step 2] Add doors and windows by selecting the direction of each then dragging into place.
  • [Step 3] Outline the entire floor plan to indicate the house’s boundary.
  • [Step 4] Describe your home layout in the Text panel on the right.
  • [Step 5] Press Save All to download PNG/JSON/TXT files to upload into Google Form.
Note
  • The human icon represents a height of 180 cm.
  • To remove a room/door/window/boundary, select it and press Delete.
